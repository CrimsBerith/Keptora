# TDD Evidence — Errors and Performance

Date: 2026-11-16–17  
Source plan: [errors-and-performance.plan.md](../plans/errors-and-performance.plan.md)

## User journeys covered

- As a user, opening Swipe Clean must not silently download cloud-only thumbnails.
- As a user, I must see every gallery section when months recur in noncontiguous order.
- As a user, a rejected recommendation action must not consume review allowance.
- As a user, exact duplicate groups must rely on full fingerprints, not preview-hash pruning.
- As a user, Photos cleanup history must be persisted before deletion and reflect success or cancellation.
- As a user, I can keep browsing, searching, filtering, and selecting while a scan is in progress.
