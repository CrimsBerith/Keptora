## Portfolio rejection rules
- Reject any replacement with a generic shared sidebar/card/dashboard shell.
- Reject screenshots or fixtures containing real customer/user data.
- Reject raster assets without Retina source and ownership/license evidence.
- Reject icon/screenshot substitutions that misrepresent the shipping UI.
- Final screenshot, icon visual balance, VoiceOver and contrast evidence remain real-Mac gates.
