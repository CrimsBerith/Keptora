# Cullora — App Privacy Draft

## Intended disclosure
**Data Not Collected** is the current product intent: local-first processing, no account, analytics SDK, ad SDK or cloud processing in the canonical source.

## Pre-submission proof
- Re-scan linked frameworks/SDKs in the archived build.
- Confirm no telemetry was added during Mac fixes.
- Confirm Support/Privacy web links do not transmit in-app document contents.
- Confirm StoreKit transactions are handled by Apple and no independent purchaser profile is collected by the app.

Do not select “Data Not Collected” in App Store Connect until the signed archive dependency/privacy report matches this draft.
