## 1. Local-first operation

Keptora is designed to analyze user-selected photo and media archives on the user’s Mac. The current app does not require an account and does not upload photos, videos, filenames, hashes, thumbnails, cleanup decisions, or diagnostic information to the developer.

## 2. Information processed on the device

To provide its features, Keptora may process and store locally:

- User-selected folders and files.
- File names, paths, sizes, timestamps, media type, and related metadata.
- Exact fingerprints used to identify byte-for-byte duplicates.
- On-device similarity features used to present review-only suggestions.
- Thumbnail cache files.
- Review decisions and protected-keeper choices.
- Cleanup plans, signed manifests, quarantine records, and restore history.
- Security-scoped bookmarks needed to reopen a folder the user selected.
- Preferences, onboarding completion, and the count of unique free review decisions.

This information remains on the user’s Mac in the current version.

## 3. Photos and folder permissions

Keptora requests access only when the user selects a folder or enables a supported photo-library workflow. macOS controls these permissions. Users can review or revoke access in System Settings. Revoking access can prevent scans, cleanup, or restoration until the original source is selected again.

## 4. Cleanup and restoration

Keptora does not permanently delete files from Apple Photos in the current version. For supported folder workflows, approved exact copies are moved into a reversible quarantine with an auditable manifest. Users remain responsible for reviewing the Safety Plan and maintaining backups of important data.

## 5. Purchases

A lifetime unlock may be purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment and account information under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Keptora reads verified StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether Pro features are unlocked. The developer does not receive complete payment-card information.

## 6. Diagnostics and support

Keptora can create a user-initiated JSON diagnostic export. The current export excludes image bytes and fingerprints and redacts user-home and source paths by default. The file is not sent automatically. If a user chooses to share it with support, the developer will use it only to investigate the reported issue and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for support, legal, or security purposes.

## 7. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, account system, or cross-app tracking. This policy and the App Store privacy label must be updated before any such functionality is introduced.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Local Keptora data remains on the user’s Mac until removed by an in-app workflow, by deleting the relevant local files, or by uninstalling the app and deleting its container. Quarantined files should not be removed manually before the user has completed any desired restoration. Support data voluntarily sent to the developer is handled as described in Section 6.

## 9. Security

Keptora uses the macOS App Sandbox, user-selected file permissions, local database storage, signed cleanup manifests, and conservative cleanup rules. No software can guarantee absolute security or prevent every hardware, file-system, or user error. Users should maintain independent backups of irreplaceable files.

## 10. Children

Keptora is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 11. International users

Local processing generally remains on the user’s Mac. If a user voluntarily contacts support, their message and attachments may be processed in the countries where the developer or service provider operates, subject to applicable law.

## 12. Changes

The policy may be updated when Keptora’s functionality, legal obligations, or service providers change. The published policy will show a revised effective date. Material changes should also be communicated in an appropriate release note or in-app notice.
